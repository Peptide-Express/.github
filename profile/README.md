# Peptide.Express

US supplier of research peptides with a public library of third-party CoAs and SDS. For laboratory research use only. Not for human or veterinary use.

- Website: [peptide.express](https://peptide.express)
- Certificates of analysis: [peptide.express/lab-results](https://peptide.express/lab-results)
- LinkedIn: [linkedin.com/company/peptide-express](https://www.linkedin.com/company/peptide-express)
- X: [@Peptide_Express](https://x.com/Peptide_Express)
- Reddit: [u/PeptideExpress](https://www.reddit.com/user/PeptideExpress)

## Repositories

- [peptide-reference-data](https://github.com/Peptide-Express/peptide-reference-data): reference masses for 23 research peptides and a Python mass calculator for checking the identity section of a CoA.
